InternVL3-8B functional joint validation on the frozen 3,000-question GQA cohort (six tasks, 500 questions each; 2,409 image-SHA clusters). A: All-Writer, R-to-image enabled in L0-L27; B: R-to-image enabled only in L0-L19; C: A with the o_proj attention output zeroed for P-1 and true autoregressive Decode queries in L2-L5; D: B with the same output zeroing. In all four conditions, P-1 and Decode cannot directly attend to original image keys in any layer, while image queries, non-target queries, norms, MLPs, residual additions and dense KV caches are preserved. Panel (a) shows full-answer GQA accuracy. Panel (b) shows paired accuracy changes and ordinary, unadjusted 95% confidence intervals from 20,000 whole-image-cluster bootstrap resamples. The gray vertical line marks zero, and the orange dotted line marks the descriptive -1 pp quality reference. The L20 boundary was selected after earlier results were known; these exploratory intervals do not establish independently confirmed noninferiority. Output zeroing is a functional intervention and does not demonstrate physical attention skipping or acceleration.
