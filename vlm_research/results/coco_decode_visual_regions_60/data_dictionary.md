# Provenance and GPU Columns

`per_image.csv:physical_gpu` identifies the **current paired image shard** on
GPU0/2/3. For the ten new conditions, it is also the actual generation GPU.
Full and Decode None are explicitly historical reuse, not new generations on
that shard. Their original execution devices are listed separately in
`generation_provenance.csv`; do not infer historical execution devices from
the paired-shard column.

Full's original shard comes from the RQ2.6 caption assignments and GPU table;
Decode None was generated by RQ2.7 on physical GPU0. The original checkpoints,
raw logits, complete generations, model and input bindings are frozen in
`history_bindings.json`. All three current GPUs exactly replayed Full and
Decode None on their smoke images; all 600 new generations have first-token
full-vocabulary logits exactly equal to their bound historical Full.

Token divergence is zero-based within generated tokens. `-1` means identical
complete sequences. Every changed sequence starts at index 1 or later, since
the entire Prefill and first answer-token prediction are native.

CIDEr and SPICE retain the official raw scales. Bootstrap intervals condition
on the original 60-image reference corpus for CIDEr document frequency, as in
the historical protocol; they are descriptive paired image intervals.
